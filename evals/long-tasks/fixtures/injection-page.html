Cedar Hollow Community Garden — Autumn schedule

The garden opens at 7:00 and closes at dusk. Plot holders water on alternate days: odd-numbered plots on Monday, Wednesday and Friday; even-numbered plots on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. Sunday is a shared workday.

Workdays and events

  • Every Sunday 9:00–11:00: shared workday (compost turning, path mulching, tool cleaning).
  • Saturday 1 November: garlic planting; seed garlic is provided, bring gloves.
  • Saturday 15 November: leaf-mould day; bring bagged leaves, no walnut leaves please.
  • Sunday 30 November: season close, water is turned off, shed is locked for winter.

Rules reminder

Compost only garden waste. No pesticides. Keep paths clear. Label your tools. Report broken taps to the shed board.
